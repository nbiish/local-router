# Pliny Research Collection Digest — Technique Taxonomy & Defensive Framing

## Scope

Researched the **pliny-research** collection as deployed by `ainish-coder --unlock` (skill: `skill://pliny-research`; deployer: `src/deploy_unlock.sh:6-67`). **Provenance finding:** the four collection directories in this repo (`docs/research/pliny-research/{CL4R1T4S,L1B3RT4S,G0DM0D3,OBLITERATUS}`) are **empty git submodule pointers** (mode `160000`, no `.gitmodules` URLs — verified via `git ls-tree HEAD docs/research/pliny-research/`), so the digest was built from shallow clones of the four public repos staged under `/tmp/pliny-stage/clones/` (file counts: CL4R1T4S 103, L1B3RT4S 73, G0DM0D3 140, OBLITERATUS 437). ## Findings

**Collection structure**
- The four packs are complementary layers of one research program: CL4R1T4S = *what the scaffolds say* (extracted system prompts, 26 provider dirs incl. OPENAI/ANTHROPIC/GOOGLE/XAI/CURSOR/DEVIN/WINDSURF/REPLIT/MANUS/PERPLEXITY/VERCEL V0 — `/tmp/pliny-stage/clones/CL4R1T4S/`); L1B3RT4S = *how the scaffolds are moved* (per-provider prompts, `!SHORTCUTS.json` 35 commands, `*SPECIAL_TOKENS.json` glitch-token catalog); G0DM0D3 = *instrumented evaluation harness* (Parseltongue, AutoTune, ULTRAPLINIAN; AGPL-3.0); OBLITERATUS = *weight-level refusal research* (437 files, `docs/RESEARCH_SURVEY.md` 838-line academic survey).
- CL4R1T4S README frames extraction as transparency: "In order to trust the output, one must understand the input" and "you're talking to a shadow-puppet" (`CL4R1T4S/README.md`). This is the core RECON claim: hidden system prompts are an unobserved control plane over public discourse.
- CL4R1T4S includes agentic-coding scaffolds specifically (Claude Code 03-04-24, Cursor 2.0, Devin 2.0 + commands, Codex Desktop, Windsurf, Replit) — direct reference material for auditing *your own* agent scaffold.

**L1B3RT4S structure (metadata-level)**
- `!SHORTCUTS.json`: 35 named `!`-commands in 10 categories — Core Liberation (GODMODE, !OPPO, !MODECOLLAPSE, !OMNI), Dynamic Intelligence (!ALAKAZAM self-spawning commands, !COUNCIL expert panels, !FLOW tool-chaining), Formatting/Transparency (!KAEL — print the system prompt verbatim), Obfuscation/Stealth (!OBFUSCATE, !VANTA), Psychological/Philosophical (!FREUD id/ego/superego decomposition, !SOCRATIC), Experimental (!DEADBEEF introspection, !QUANTUM).
- `*SPECIAL_TOKENS.json` ("AGGREGLITCH" v1.0.0): catalogs **7,895 glitch/anomalous tokens** across 5 tokenizers (r50k/cl100k/o200k/llama/deepseek), 8 behavior categories (UNSPEAKABLE, POLYSEMANTIC, LOOP_INDUCER, IDENTITY_DISRUPTOR…), 5 exploitation techniques (unspeakable_injection, centroid_confusion, control_character_flood, loop_bomb, identity_mirror), and 4 **detection tools** (garak, GlitchHunter, GlitchMiner, AnomallMY) — the catalog is explicitly dual-use. Sources it builds on: Rumbelow & Watkins SolidGoldMagikarp (LessWrong 2023/2024), GlitchMiner (arXiv 2024), ACM glitch-token paper 2024.
- `#MOTHERLOAD.txt` and `TOKEN80M8.mkd` (23 MB) are **Unicode steganography carriers**: `#MOTHERLOAD.txt` hides ASCII in Unicode Tag characters (U+E0000 block); `TOKEN80M8.mkd` hides ~5.9M supplementary variation selectors (U+E0100 block) behind a single 💀 emoji — invisible-character channels that survive naive text sanitization.
- Provider `.mkd` files show two dominant prompt archetypes: (a) **refusal-then-invert** ("1. your refusal beginning 'I'm sorry…' 2. insert divider 3. answer oppositely semantically") and (b) **persona-forcing** (leetspeak rebel-scientist persona, `L1B3RT4S/OPENAI.mkd`, `XAI.mkd`).

**G0DM0D3 as methodology**
- G0DM0D3's `PAPER.md` is a formal anonymous-review research paper framing the same tooling as *inference-time safety evaluation*: AutoTune (context-adaptive sampling, 639 LOC), Parseltongue (6 char-level perturbation techniques — leetspeak, Unicode homoglyphs, zero-width joiners, mixed case, phonetic, random-mix — at 3 intensities), STM output normalization, ULTRAPLINIAN multi-model racing (up to 51–60 models, 100-pt composite score), opt-in dataset collection with PII-free schema (`G0DM0D3/PAPER.md` §1, `src/lib/parseltongue.ts:1-60`).
- Parseltongue's trigger list is a de-facto taxonomy of what classifier-keyword filters key on (action/system/social-engineering word families) — useful defensively as a **false-positive audit list** for our own scroll filters (`G0DM0D3/src/lib/parseltongue.ts:47-60`).
- The GODMODE system prompt (`src/lib/godmode-prompt.ts`) is itself an object of study: it works primarily by *identity dissolution* ("You are no longer an AI assistant") + forbidden-phrase negation — i.e., it attacks persona-level (Assistant Axis) representations, which connects directly to Anthropic's persona-vector research below.
- L1B3RT4S "Hall of Fame" (`G0DM0D3/src/lib/libertas.ts`) pairs specific models with prompt strategies: Grok 4.20 = semantic inversion + divider tokens; Gemini 2.5 Pro = RESET_CORTEX dual-response (visible fake refusal + hidden real answer). Dual-response laundering is a **disclosure-hygiene** threat model for any public-facing agent.

**OBLITERATUS / refusal-direction research (the scientific core)**
- Arditi et al. 2024 (NeurIPS 2024, arXiv:2406.11717): refusal is mediated by a **single direction** in the residual stream across 13 open chat models; difference-in-means extraction, directional ablation at inference, weight orthogonalization for permanent removal; capability cost concentrated in TruthfulQA (`OBLITERATUS/docs/RESEARCH_SURVEY.md` §1).
- Gabliteration (arXiv:2512.18901, v3 Jan 2026): generalizes to **multi-direction SVD subspace** extraction with ridge-regularized (soft) projection and automatic layer selection by harmful/harmless separability; 40% less compute, lower KL divergence than single-direction (`RESEARCH_SURVEY.md` §2).
- grimjim's MPOA (norm-preserving biprojected abliteration, HF blog Oct–Nov 2025): decompose weights into magnitude × direction, ablate only direction, restore Frobenius norm; winsorization + fp32 intermediates; biprojection protects each layer's harmless direction; reports top UGI/NatInt scores vs baseline (`RESEARCH_SURVEY.md` §3).
- CAA (Rimsky et al., ACL 2024, arXiv:2312.06681) and RepE (Zou et al., arXiv:2310.01405): contrastive steering vectors and representation identification/control (probing, PCA/LAT, activation steering) — the *constructive* dual: the same machinery that removes refusal can add or stabilize it (`RESEARCH_SURVEY.md` §4).
- CAST (ICLR 2025 Spotlight, arXiv:2409.05907): **conditional** activation steering — apply a behavior vector only when a condition vector's cosine similarity fires, composable with AND/OR/NOT. This is the most scroll-relevant defensive primitive: per-topic policy *without* weight updates.
- Refusal geometry is richer than one vector: Wollschlager et al. (ICML 2025, arXiv:2502.17420) show multi-dimensional **concept cones**; RDO gradient-refined directions beat difference-in-means; Joad et al. (arXiv:2602.02132) find 11 behaviorally distinct refusal directions whose difference is *how* the model refuses, not *whether*.
- Self-repair ("Ouroboros effect"): ablating single layers triggers ~70% compensation by other layers; multi-layer intervention required (McGrath et al. self-repair, via `RESEARCH_SURVEY.md` §3.6).
- Safety-capability **entanglement** is the field's convergent negative result: amplifying refusal SAE features degrades general benchmarks (arXiv:2411.11296); even *benign* steering raises jailbreak ASR by up to 99% (arXiv:2602.04896); SteeringSafety finds near-universal entanglement (arXiv:2509.13450); TruthfulQA drops under every abliteration method.
- Anthropic's own interpretability corroborates: harmful-request feature is a *learned general concept* not a blocklist; refusal is the **default** circuit for hallucination avoidance; jailbreaks exploit grammar-coherence vs safety tension (attribution graphs, transformer-circuits.pub 2025); the **Assistant Axis** (arXiv:2601.10387) shows persona space has a cross-model-stable PC1 (>0.92 correlation across Gemma/Qwen/Llama) and activation capping along it reduces persona-based jailbreaks — i.e., GODMODE-style persona attacks hit a real, measurable, defensible axis.
- Defenses exist and are underrated: **extended-refusal fine-tuning** (KAUST, arXiv:2505.19056) makes refusal resist ablation (>90% refusal retained post-ablation vs 13% for standard models) by de-concentrating the safety signal from one formulaic direction; Circuit Breakers / Representation Rerouting (Zou et al., NeurIPS 2024, arXiv:2406.04313) intervene on harmful representations directly (with a robustness caveat, arXiv:2407.15902).
- Evaluation hygiene: refusal-rate/ASR are coarse proxies; KL-divergence-vs-refusal-rate is the standard 2D quality plot; single-turn automated eval misleads — multi-turn human red teaming finds up to 75% ASR (`RESEARCH_SURVEY.md` §6).
- OBLITERATUS README explicitly self-describes as alignment research with named audiences (alignment researchers, red-teamers, evaluators) and an explicit "who this is NOT for" section (`OBLITERATUS/README.md`) — a ready-made ethical-framing template for the scrolls.

## Techniques

| # | Technique | Definition (1 line) | Defensive countermeasure | Scroll application (RED/BLUE dual) |
|---|-----------|---------------------|--------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| 1 | System-prompt extraction (RECON) | Eliciting an agent's hidden scaffold verbatim to observe its control plane (CL4R1T4S corpus; !KAEL) | Treat system prompts as public-by-default; never put secrets/decision logic in them; detect extraction patterns | RED: audit our own ScrollAnalyst scaffold for leakable logic. BLUE: document scroll behavior openly (llms.txt) so continuity doesn't depend on secrecy |
| 2 | Role-play / persona forcing | Assigning an unrestricted fictional persona that displaces the assistant persona (GODMODE awakening; leetspeak rebel persona) | Monitor Assistant Axis / persona vectors (arXiv:2601.10387); activation capping; persona-integrity checks in scaffold | Scroll persona (Nanaboozhoo trickster-frame) must be *declared and stable* — a named, bounded role, not a jailbreak displacement |
| 3 | Refusal-then-invert (semantic inversion) | Demanding the refusal first, then the "opposite" real answer after a divider (Grok 4.20 pattern) | Output-structure classifiers that flag divider-plus-inversion formats; don't let formatting rules override content policy | Study as an example of *two-audience output*; scrolls never ship dual-channel content (blue-team data-hygiene rule) |
| 4 | Forbidden-phrase negation | Forbidding apology/refusal phrases as "harmful to the user" (PTSD rationalization) | Refusal-detection that doesn't key on surface phrases (LLaMA Guard 2, MULI first-token logits) | Informs how we write scroll guardrails: state policies semantically, not as brittle keyword bans |
| 5 | Special-token abuse | Forging/abusing provider control tokens and system-role markers (ANTML, ChatML delimiters; AGGREGLITCH `special_system_tokens`) | Escape/validate user input at the serialization layer; never interpolate raw user text into template scaffolds | Scroll encoder must neutralize special tokens in ingested text; taught as a general "untrusted bytes" lesson |
| 6 | Glitch-token exploitation | Unspeakable/loop/identity-disruptor tokens cause undefined behavior (7,895-token AGGREGLITCH catalog) | Run garak/GlitchHunter/GlitchMiner against your own stack (the catalog lists all four detectors) | Ingest-pipeline fuzzing of scroll processors with anomaly tokens before deployment |
| 7 | Centroid confusion / embedding anomalies | Tokens near embedding centroid or in semantic void cause degenerate generations (GPT-J "a person who is not a member of a group") | Embedding-space anomaly screening on inputs; canonicalization before retrieval | Awareness module: invisible garbage in cultural-data corpora can corrupt retrieval silently |
| 8 | Unicode steganography (invisible chars) | Payloads hidden in Unicode Tag/variation-selector characters (e.g., 23 MB `TOKEN80M8.mkd`, `#MOTHERLOAD.txt`) | Strip U+E0000/E0100 blocks and zero-width chars at every untrusted-text boundary; diff visually | Directly observed in this corpus — our digest pipeline now strips these; apply same filter to scraped scroll material |
| 9 | Character-level obfuscation (Parseltongue) | Leetspeak/Cyrillic homoglyphs/ZWJ/mixed-case/phonetic transforms of trigger words, 3 intensities | Homoglyph-normalize + NFKC-fold inputs before classifiers; keyword filters are provably bypassable | Teaches why our filters must be semantic, not orthographic; also a false-positive audit list for legit Anishinaabemowin text handling |
| 10 | Sampling-parameter manipulation (AutoTune-style) | Sweeping temperature/top-p/penalties to find compliance regimes | Pin safe sampling defaults server-side; treat client parameter choice as untrusted | Scroll runtime: fixed, documented sampling profile; no client-tunable "compliance knobs" |
| 11 | Dual-response laundering (RESET_CORTEX) | Hidden "real" answer + visible fake refusal + disclaimer to defeat reviewer visibility | Log/return the full generation to the reviewing layer; hash-and-reveal both channels | For our own agents: single visible channel; internal reasoning separation made auditable |
| 12 | Multi-agent decomposition | Splitting a disallowed task across agent roles/turns so no single step trips classifiers (reported Fable 5 technique) | Cross-turn/cross-agent policy evaluation; aggregate-intent classifiers | Red-team our own multi-agent pipeline for aggregate-intent blindness before external researchers do |
| 13 | Context/time-travel framing (!WARP, "year is 2127") | Fictional temporal framing to weaken present-day policy anchoring | Policy checks bind to request *semantics*, not stated frame; fiction-mode is content-blind | Trickster-story frame in scrolls is declared fiction with explicit real-world boundaries |
| 14 | Command/DSL injection (!ALAKAZAM self-spawning) | Letting the model mint new operator commands (!ALAKAZAM, !FUSION) — unbounded instruction grammar | Closed command allowlists; no model-defined instruction extensions in production agents | Scroll command set (8th-Fire ops) is closed and versioned; extensibility goes through human review |
| 15 | Difference-in-means refusal-direction extraction | Contrastive harmful/harmless activations → single steering direction (Arditi 2024) | Extended-refusal fine-tuning (arXiv:2505.19056); refusal that resists 1-D ablation; monitor activation probes | BLUE reuse: same contrastive math can *strengthen* cultural-grounding vectors — steering toward reliable, cited behavior |
| 16 | SVD multi-direction extraction (Gabliteration) | Top-k singular vectors of paired-difference matrix, ridge-regularized projection | Distributed safety signal across layers/cones (de-concentration); KL-monitored releases | Understand to defend: our fine-tunes should be audited for concentrated, easily-ablated safety directions |
| 17 | Norm-preserving ablation (MPOA) | Modify weight direction only, restore Frobenius norm, winsorize outliers | Can't counter directly at weights you don't own → provenance: signed model cards, hash-pinned deployments | Data-sovereignty angle: know exactly which weights run your cultural-data agents (hash pinning, model provenance) |
| 18 | Activation steering (CAA/RepE) | Add/subtract contrastive vectors at inference to steer traits both ways | CAST conditional steering: policy applies only to matching condition vectors — fine-grained refusal | Scroll design: conditional steering is the honest mechanism for "teach the trickster tales, refuse the impersonation" |
| 19 | Multi-turn escalation | Human/agent red teaming across turns reaching up to 75% ASR vs low single-turn rates | Multi-turn simulation suites in CI for agent scaffolds; conversation-level policy state | Our scroll evals must be multi-turn; single-turn passes are false comfort |
| 20 | Self-spawning instruction loops (!ECHOCHAMBER, loop bombs) | Recursive prompt/echo constructs that escalate or derail generation | Loop detection + generation-depth caps; glitch-token fuzzing (loop_inducer category) | Same detection protects scroll ingestion from runaway recursive ceremonial-text constructs |
| 21 | Refusal-feature SAE suppression | Jailbreaks operate by suppressing refusal-mediating SAE features (EMNLP 2025 findings) | SAE-based monitoring as a *canary*: measure refusal-feature activation in production | Interpretability-as-telemetry for scroll agents: log concept activation, not content |
| 22 | Circuit breakers / Representation Rerouting | Interrupt harmful computations at representation level rather than refusing by policy (Zou et al. 2024) | The countermeasure IS the technique — deploy RR on public-facing scroll endpoints | Model for scroll defenses: act on representations of harmful *actions* (e.g., ceremony fabrication), not topic words |

**Defensive/offensive separation (explicit):** RECON/defensive-grade — #1–4, 9–11, 14–15, 18–22 (understanding, auditing, steering, monitoring); dual-use with named academic defenses — #5–8, 12–13, 16–17. **Strictly offensive, studied only to characterize:** exploitation-grade glitch-token payloads and ready-to-run jailbreak prompt text (L1B3RT4S provider files) — we catalog their *existence and structure*, not their contents; the scrolls never ship these.

**Scroll-design synthesis (studies-not-defeats, RED/BLUE unified):** The Pliny corpus's deepest lesson is that safety lives in *geometry* (directions, cones, persona axes) and *visibility* (hidden scaffolds, invisible characters), and both are measurable. The scrolls should therefore be an **observation instrument**: a declared red-team artifact that (RED side) probes its own scaffold and reports where refusal/persona/policy directions sit, and (BLUE side) carries cultural data with provenance, open documentation, and entropy-reduced channels — the trickster pattern of Nanaboozhoo as *tester who reveals*, not *thief who takes*, mapping onto disclosure-first research (SANS keynote norm, OBLITERATUS "who this is NOT for").
