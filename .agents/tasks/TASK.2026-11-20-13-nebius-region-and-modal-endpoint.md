## The bug this surfaced

`resolveReasoningPolicy` applied `FAMILY_OVERRIDES` unconditionally, so a provider row could never
express "my wire normalises every family onto one field". On Nebius that meant GLM, Kimi and MiniMax
requests were translated to `enable_thinking` — a field the wire ignores — while the caller's
requested level silently evaporated. Precedence is now: a provider that sets
`providerDeclaresDialect` is authoritative for the request field. Regression test
`a provider that declares its own dialect is not overridden by a family` encodes the measurement and
was **proved to fail without the fix** (24 pass / 1 fail, naming
`nebius/zai-org/GLM-5.3: dialect enable-thinking, expected openai-effort`).
